How F gets a gradient with no gradient library. A linear gradient that never animates is just a picture. Export it once at 2×512px, ship it in the update, stretch it behind the hero; same for the header bloom. EAS Update carries assets, not only JavaScript, so this costs zero build credits. What it cannot do is respond to touch, animate between colours, or mask to an arbitrary shape — which is exactly the line between F and G.
G cannot ship over the air, and that is not a detail. It needs expo-blur, expo-linear-gradient and react-native-svg — three native modules, so two new store binaries and another Apple review on an app that just cleared one after a rejection cycle. Nobody sees it until they update. F reaches every existing user within hours and rolls back by republishing the previous update. If you want G, the sane sequencing is to ship F now and fold the native pieces into the next scheduled binary.
