Classic carries its own colours, and that resolves the contradiction. Earlier rounds held the look axis and palette axis fully independent, which broke the moment every new candidate became dark. Fixed: each look owns a palette family. Switching to Classic restores the 12px radii, the 48px numeral, the flat cards and the navy — which is what a user means by “revert” anyway. The brief has been revised to match and both gates re-run.
Two things I am assuming until you say otherwise. First, Timber is the default for new installs — your own CLAUDE.md names livestock owners, hunters and outdoor workers, and Blaze is the only one of the two that narrows the app to hunting alone. Blaze remains one tap away. Second, existing users stay on Classic through the update and are offered Aurora once, rather than waking up to a dark app they never asked for. Both are one-word reversals and neither is frozen yet.
This is no longer an over-the-air change. Aurora needs expo-blur, expo-linear-gradient and react-native-svg: two new store binaries and another Apple review, on an app that just cleared one after a rejection cycle. The palette itself stays free to edit over the air afterwards. Unchanged: app/_layout.tsx and UpdateCheckScreen.tsx keep hardcoded brand navy to match the native splash, and radar.tsx line 65 carries a Mapbox-native style prop the migration must skip.
